1. Background

This Order Form is entered into under, and is governed by, the Master Subscription Agreement dated December 12, 2024 between Provider and Customer (the "MSA"). Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings given in the MSA.

2. Subscribed Services

Provider will make the Meridian Fleet Telematics Platform (SKU MRD-FLEET-SEAT) available to Customer for each vehicle seat activated by Customer (each, an "Active Seat").

3. Fees

3.1 Standard Subscription Fee

Customer shall pay a subscription fee of $250.00 per Active Seat per month (the "Base Rate").

3.2 Introductory Promotional Rate

As an onboarding incentive, Customer shall pay a reduced rate of $200.00 per Active Seat per month (the "Promotional Rate") for all billing periods through and including June 30, 2025. The Promotional Rate shall expire automatically on June 30, 2025, and the Base Rate shall apply to all billing periods thereafter without further notice.

3.3 Annual Price Escalator

Commencing January 1, 2026, and on each anniversary of that date during the Term, all per-seat Fees shall increase by five percent (5.0%) over the rate in effect immediately prior to such date.

3.4 Monthly Minimum Commitment

Notwithstanding the number of Active Seats in any month, Customer shall pay a minimum monthly fee of $12,000.00 (the "Monthly Minimum"). If the per-seat Fees calculated for a month are less than the Monthly Minimum, Provider shall invoice the Monthly Minimum for that month.

4. Invoicing and Payment

4.1 Fees are invoiced monthly in advance on the first day of each billing period.

4.2 Payment is due net thirty (30) days from the invoice date.

4.3 Overdue amounts accrue a late charge of 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is less.

5. Taxes

Fees are exclusive of all sales, use, and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay.
